{# The single most important thing on this page is what it does NOT do. #}

A flag is not a verdict.

Silence is not proof a ride was abandoned. A legitimate trip can cross a tunnel, sit in a basement car park, run for hours in traffic, be backgrounded by the phone, or end with a flat battery while the driver is still carrying the passenger. Nothing on this page has changed any ride's status, fare or settlement.

Judge each row: a normal long ride keeps moving and reappears healthy on its own; a temporary disconnect clears itself the moment the driver returns; a likely abandoned ride stays silent and keeps climbing.
